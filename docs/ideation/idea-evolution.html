Step 2
Filtered against
Four questions, in this order
  1. Does it address the gap nobody covers? Re-planning appears in the brief and in almost no shipping product.
  2. Can four people actually ship it in the time, alongside coursework?
  3. Does it need money we don’t have — a paid API, a subscription, a merchant account?
  4. Will it survive a live demo with flaky wifi and no API keys?
Question 3 killed more ideas than anything else, and question 4 is why the AI call has a key-free mock mode.
