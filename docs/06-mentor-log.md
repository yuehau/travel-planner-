## Questions worth asking

Ask questions where the answer would actually change what we build. Ranked:

1. **Is the disruption-recovery wedge real, or are we solving a problem people just absorb?**
   The whole thesis rests on this. If a mentor with travel or product experience says
   people simply shrug and re-plan in the group chat without much pain, we need to know
   before Friday, not after.

2. **Does the tradeoff panel land, or does it feel like the app is blaming someone?**
   "Sarah's budget cap was the binding constraint" is either the most useful thing in the
   product or a social landmine. We genuinely do not know which. Show a mentor the mockup
   and watch their face.

3. **Is three recovery options the right number?** Two may feel thin, five overwhelming.
   Cheap to change now, expensive on stage.

4. **What breaks this in front of judges?** Ask directly. Mentors have watched more
   pitches fail than we have given.

5. **Is our scope honest?** We cut live booking APIs, offline, and real-time collab. Does
   cutting those make the demo feel like a toy, or like a focused prototype?
