A slow-bar coffee counter just opened behind Telawi

Twelve seats, no laptops, and a rotating list of Sabah-grown beans. Bangsar's newest café wants you to sit still for twenty minutes.

Tucked into a former tailor's shop on Lorong Kurau, the new counter keeps things deliberately small: twelve stools around a single brew bar, a two-page menu, and a house rule that laptops stay in the bag.

The beans are the story. The owners source most of the list from smallholders around Ranau and Tenom in Sabah, roasting on-site every Tuesday. Expect a bright washed Ranau on pour-over and a heavier natural for the espresso drinks.

Pastries come from a home baker in Bukit Damansara and sell out by 11am on weekends. Go on a weekday afternoon, order the seasonal tasting flight, and let the barista talk you through it.
